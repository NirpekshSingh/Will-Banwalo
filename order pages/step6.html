Helpful Tips

01

High Resolution

Ensure text is readable without zooming. Avoid blurry phone camera shots.

02

Full Visibility

Don't crop out the edges of the document. Keep all four corners visible.

03

File Formats

PDF is preferred for documents, while JPG/PNG works best for photos.
