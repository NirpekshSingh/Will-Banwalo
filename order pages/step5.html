Legal Tips

Be Specific

Instead of "my car," use "my 2022 Toyota Camry, Registration ABC-1234." This prevents disputes during probate.

Guardian Selection

Always discuss the role with your chosen guardians first. Ensure they share your values and have the financial capacity to raise children.

Funeral Expenses

Consider setting aside a specific 'Funeral Fund' in your asset portfolio to ensure your final wishes are carried out without financial burden.
