Our Story

About Will Banwalo

At Will Banwalo, we believe that securing your family's future should be simple, accessible, and deeply trustworthy.

We are building a modern estate planning experience for India — where legal expertise meets digital convenience. Our platform allows individuals and families to create legally valid Wills from the comfort of their homes, without the complexity, delays, or intimidation of traditional legal processes.

Our Mission

To make estate planning effortless for every Indian household.

We aim to remove the barriers that prevent people from creating a Will — whether it's lack of time, confusion around legal procedures, or the discomfort of visiting legal chambers. With Will Banwalo, you can plan your legacy in minutes, not months.

Why We Exist

In India, millions of families face unnecessary disputes, delays, and stress simply because a Will was never created.

We exist to change that.

By combining technology with legal expertise, we ensure that every individual — regardless of background — has access to a secure, structured, and legally compliant way to protect their loved ones.
