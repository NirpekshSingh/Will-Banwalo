Compliance & Legal Framework

At Will Banwalo, compliance is not an afterthought — it is the foundation of everything we build.

We operate at the intersection of law and technology, ensuring that every Will created on our platform adheres to applicable Indian legal standards while maintaining the highest levels of data protection and user trust.

1. Legal Compliance

Our platform is designed in alignment with Indian legal frameworks governing Wills and succession.

  • All Will formats are structured in accordance with the Indian Succession Act, 1925
  • Users are guided through legally required elements such as testator details, beneficiaries, executors, and asset distribution
  • Clear instructions are provided for proper execution, including witnessing and signing requirements

While registration of a Will is optional under Indian law, we provide guidance to users who choose to register their document.

2. Lawyer Review Standards

To maintain legal integrity:

  • Wills (under applicable plans) are reviewed by qualified legal professionals
  • Each document is checked for structural accuracy and compliance
  • Ambiguities or inconsistencies are flagged for correction

This ensures that your document meets practical legal standards before finalization.

3. Data Protection & Privacy Compliance

We follow strict data protection practices to safeguard user information:

  • Encryption protocols are applied to sensitive data
  • Access to user data is restricted and controlled
  • Systems are designed to prevent unauthorized access or misuse

We align our practices with applicable Indian data protection laws and evolving digital privacy standards.

4. Digital Security Standards

Security is built into every layer of our platform:

  • Secure data transmission and storage systems
  • Controlled authentication and access management
  • Continuous monitoring to detect and prevent threats

Our goal is to ensure that your personal and financial information remains protected at all times.

5. Financial & Transaction Compliance

Where applicable:

  • Payments are processed through compliant and secure payment gateways
  • Transactions follow applicable Indian financial regulations
  • User payment data is not stored directly on our systems (where third-party gateways are used)

6. Platform Integrity & Ethical Use

We maintain strict standards to ensure ethical and lawful use of our platform:

  • Users must provide accurate and lawful information
  • Fraudulent or misleading use of the platform is prohibited
  • We reserve the right to restrict access in case of misuse or violations

7. Limitations of Platform Scope

While we ensure compliance and structured legal workflows:

  • Will Banwalo is a technology-enabled platform, not a substitute for full legal representation
  • Complex legal disputes, inheritance conflicts, or litigation matters may require independent legal advice

8. Continuous Compliance Monitoring

We regularly review and update our systems to stay aligned with:

  • Changes in Indian laws and regulations
  • Evolving data protection standards
  • Industry best practices in legal technology

9. User Responsibility in Compliance

Users play an important role in ensuring compliance:

  • Providing accurate and complete information
  • Following execution guidelines (signatures, witnesses, etc.)
  • Reviewing their Will periodically as circumstances change
