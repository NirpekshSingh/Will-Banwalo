Clarifying your doubts

Absolute validity is ensured by compliance with the Indian Succession Act, 1925. Our digital workflow guides you through the necessary witnessing and signing protocols that make it legally binding.

While optional under Indian law, registration offers an additional layer of security. We provide complete guidance if you choose to pursue registration at your local sub-registrar office.

Life is dynamic. Our platform allows you to update your Will seamlessly as your assets or family structure evolves. We recommend a review every 2-3 years.
